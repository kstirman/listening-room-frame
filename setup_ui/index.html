LISTENING ROOM

Your music. On your Frame.

Connect your display, choose a room, and let the artwork follow.

Connecting to setup…

Your music continues through your existing speakers.

01

Connect your Frame

Keep the TV in Art Mode. You may need to approve a connection on its screen.

Discovery stays on your local network.

If discovery finds nothing, use the address in your TV’s network settings. Other Samsung TVs may be discovered; only compatible Frame Art Mode models work.

02

Choose what to follow

Follows this room even when it joins a group. Music still plays through Sonos.

First install and authorize the optional extension and choose its output using the Roon guide in this project. This page does not pair Roon or choose its output.

Use the endpoint from your renderer’s device description. Advanced library enrichment settings can be supplied through the API.

Saving does not start playback or change the TV.

03

Make it yours

Save your choices first. Check the connection, then show a test image or start following music.

The test image returns to your original art after 15 seconds. Stop any other artwork app controlling this TV before testing or starting.

↻

Keep it running

This computer needs to remain on. You can close the browser; keep the setup process running.

Linux user service. Startup without login needs an additional system setting; reboot verification is still required.
