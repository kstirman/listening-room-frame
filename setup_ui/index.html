01 / THE CANVAS

Let’s find your Frame.

Put the TV in Art Mode—the mode that displays artwork when you aren’t watching TV. Keep this computer and the Frame on the same home network.

We’ll look for your TV and Sonos rooms. Nothing will start playing.

I don’t see my TV
TV settings→Network→IP address

On the TV, look under Settings → Connection or General → Network → Network Status → IP Settings. Labels vary by model. Enter the IP address here. Samsung’s illustrated guide ↗. Avoid guest Wi-Fi, which can keep devices apart.

We verify this model before sending artwork. Discovering a Samsung TV doesn’t guarantee Art Mode compatibility.

02 / THE SOUNDTRACK

Where are you listening?

Choose the system playing your music. We’ll follow its artwork and track information. Your speakers and music controls stay exactly where they are.

If this room joins a group, we follow the group’s music. We don’t change grouping or volume.

First install and authorize the Roon extension and choose its output using the project’s Roon guide. This page reads that extension; it doesn’t pair Roon.

Use the endpoint from the renderer’s device description. Advanced library settings can be supplied through the API.

04 / DROP THE NEEDLE

Put something on.

Open your usual music app and play a track in the room you chose. Then prepare its artwork here—without sending it to the TV.

You’ll see the real generated image in Now Playing.

A quick listening check
  1. Does the cover match the album?
  2. Skip to the next track. Preview again and check the title.
  3. Start the display from Now Playing, then check the Frame itself.

Some radio, line-in, TV audio and streaming sources don’t supply complete artwork or titles. Missing information stays missing—we don’t invent it.

05 / STAY A WHILE

Keep the music on the wall.

This computer does the artwork work. It needs to stay on and connected, but you can close the browser.

For today

Keep the setup process running. Start the display from Now Playing when you’re ready.

For every listening session

On Linux, enable the user service. Startup without login needs an additional host setting and a reboot check.

Stopping the display returns control to your original artwork. We follow one selected music source at a time.
